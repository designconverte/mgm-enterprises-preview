    Questions

    What people ask first.

    Call or text (508) 360-5151
    How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

    It depends on the size of the room, the layout and your selections, so your number comes after the visit. As a reference, a full bathroom remodel rarely comes in under $30,000. Gabriel walks you through your estimate, line by line: what goes into materials and what goes into labor.

    Why isn't your price the lowest?

    Because we don't try to win on price. Gabriel walks you through your number: what goes into materials and what goes into labor. A quote far below the others usually has a reason, like steps skipped along the way or no insurance. We'd rather explain our price than cut corners to lower it.

    How long does a bathroom remodel take?

    Every bathroom is different, so your timeline comes with your estimate. The exact dates are set before work starts, once every selection is made. Then each trade comes in on its turn, and the timeline we give you has room built in, so it's a date you can plan around.

    It's a rental or a vacation home, and I'm not there. Can you still do it?

    Yes. We plan access with you, whether that's a lockbox or a spare key, and you get the schedule before work starts. Gabriel texts you updates with photos as the work moves along. Tell us about rental dates or anything else we should plan around.

    Why does a kitchen with custom cabinets take longer?

    Because custom cabinets are built to order in a cabinet shop, and the kitchen can't be finished until they arrive. We plan the schedule around them from the start. Many clients remodel the kitchen together with a bathroom, so both can be planned together.

    Who will be working in my home?

    Gabriel runs every project. The work is done by trusted subcontractors (plumber, electrician, tile installer and painter), each scheduled on its own turn, so you know who's coming and when.

    Are you registered and insured?

    Yes. MGM Enterprises is insured, and we hold Massachusetts Home Improvement Contractor Registration #655026. You can look the registration up on the state's public registry.

    How does the estimate work?

    It's free. It starts with a visit to the home: Gabriel looks at the space and listens to what you have in mind. Your estimate arrives 24 to 48 hours after the visit. Want to see your new bathroom before it's built? A 3D design is available as a paid add-on.

    Is my town in your area?

    Most likely. We work across Cape Cod, from Bourne and Falmouth out to the Outer Cape, and on the South Shore, from Plymouth up to Hingham and Hull. Enter your ZIP in the form. If the home is outside our area, we'll tell you.
